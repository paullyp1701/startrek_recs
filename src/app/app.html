Where to Start Trek

Welcome! "Where to Start Trek" is a guide for exploring the Star Trek franchise, whether you're a newcomer looking for the perfect place to begin or a longtime fan searching for standout episodes. The site features curated recommendations designed to introduce new viewers to the franchise, alongside season-by-season selections of recommended episodes from each of the major Star Trek series. Check out the following entries, which contains a description of the episode, an explanation for why the episode might be appealing, and a link to IMDb by clicking on the image. Whether you're taking your first steps into the final frontier or revisiting a familiar voyage, this guide will help you decide where to start.
